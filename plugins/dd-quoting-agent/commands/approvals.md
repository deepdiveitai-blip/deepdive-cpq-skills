---
description: Approvals waiting on you, and your quotes waiting on others
---

Follow the DD Quoting Agent rules (in the dd-cpq-quoting-agent skill) and use the dd-cpq-rep-desk skill. From
`dd_cpq_my_day`, list the approvals waiting on the rep (quote, account,
amount, why it needs approval, how long it has waited) and the rep's own
quotes waiting on someone else. For each, the rep can approve, reject (with a
comment) or recall through `dd_cpq_approval_action`: confirm each one
before acting, and read the result back.
